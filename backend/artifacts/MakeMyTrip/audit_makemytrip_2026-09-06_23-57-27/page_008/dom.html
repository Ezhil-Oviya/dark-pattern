Frequently Asked Questions - Tours and Attractions

Ques. Which international tours are best for families with kids?

Ans. Universal Studios Singapore, Tokyo DisneySea, Disneyland Paris, Legoland Malaysia and Genting SkyWorlds are the top picks for activities to do with kids. Bangkok's Safari World and Sea Life Ocean World are fantastic for younger children. MakeMyTrip offers combo tickets across multiple family attractions, so you get more for less.

Ques. What are the best international tourist attractions to visit?

Ans. The Burj Khalifa in Dubai, the Eiffel Tower and Louvre in Paris, Universal Studios and the Night Safari in Singapore and Tokyo DisneySea are among the most iconic tourist attractions in the world. All are bookable on MakeMyTrip, with skip-the-line access available for the most popular ones.

Ques. How much do Burj Khalifa tickets cost?

Ans. Ticket prices vary depending on the access level and time of visit. MakeMyTrip offers the Level 124 and 125 Fast Track Entry Ticket, with prices listed in Indian Rupees on the platform. Fast-track access is strongly recommended during peak hours when standard queues can stretch to over two hours. Check the MakeMyTrip platform for the latest pricing and availability.

Ques. What are the top things to do in Singapore?

Ans. The top things to do in Singapore include Universal Studios, the Night Safari, Gardens by the Bay, Wings of Time at Sentosa and the Singapore Oceanarium. MakeMyTrip offers combo tickets for multiple Sentosa attractions, which is perfect if you are planning a full day on the island. Book ahead - these experiences fill up fast, especially during school holidays.

Ques. What is the best time to visit Dubai attractions?

Ans. October to April is the best time to visit. Temperatures are comfortable for outdoor experiences like desert safaris and Miracle Garden. Global Village is only open from November to April, so time your trip accordingly. The Burj Khalifa and Museum of the Future are open year-round.

Ques. How do I book attraction tickets online through MakeMyTrip?

Ans. Head to the Tours & Attractions section on the MakeMyTrip app or website, select your destination and browse by category or attraction name. Most bookings come with instant confirmation. Your tickets are delivered digitally and are accessible directly from the app. Skip-the-line and fast-track options are clearly marked on each listing so you know exactly what you are getting.

Ques. Are combo tickets and multi-day passes available on MakeMyTrip?

Ans. MakeMyTrip offers combo tickets across multiple attractions in Singapore and Kuala Lumpur and multi-day passes for Tokyo DisneySea, Universal Studios Singapore, Genting SkyWorlds and Legoland Malaysia. These are significantly cheaper than buying individual tickets and are ideal if you are spending more than one day at a theme park destination.

Ques. What are the best activities to do in Bangkok?

Ans. The Chao Phraya Princess Cruise is one of the best evenings you can have in Bangkok - dinner, live performances and the city skyline on the water. Safari World is a great full-day family activity. The King Power Mahanakhon Skywalk offers the highest and most thrilling views in the city. Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World is a solid pick for families with younger children.

Ques. Is a BritRail Pass worth it for a London trip?

Ans. Absolutely, if you plan to travel outside London. BritRail Passes cover unlimited rail travel across England, Scotland and Wales, making day trips to Bath, Edinburgh and the Cotswolds both easy and affordable. Passes are available in multiple durations on MakeMyTrip, so you can pick the one that fits your itinerary.

Ques. What are the best family-friendly activities in London?

Ans. London is one of the best cities in the world for family travel. The London Eye, Madame Tussauds and the Big Bus Hop-On Hop-Off Tour are perennial favourites. Day trips to Windsor Castle and Stonehenge are consistently highly rated by Indian families visiting the UK. If you want to go further afield, BritRail Passes make travel across the country simple and cost-effective.

Ques. What should I not miss in Phuket?

Ans. The Phi Phi, Maya Bay and Khai Island Day Trip is Phuket's most-booked experience and sells out regularly - reserve it before you travel. Phuket Fantasea and Carnival Magic are both spectacular evening shows worth building your itinerary around. The James Bond Island Trip through Phang Nga Bay is breathtaking. For a premium beach day, Yona Beach Club is the top choice.

Ques. What is teamLab Planets Tokyo?

Ans. teamLab Planets is an immersive digital art museum in Tokyo's Toyosu district where you walk through massive, sensory installations - wading through shallow water, surrounded by projected flowers and cascading light. It is genuinely unlike anything else in the world and one of the most photographed experiences in Japan. It sells out weeks in advance, so book through MakeMyTrip as early as possible.

Ques. Are skip-the-line tickets available for popular attractions?

Ans. Yes. Skip-the-line access is available on MakeMyTrip for several of the world's busiest tourist attractions, including the Louvre in Paris, the Burj Khalifa in Dubai and Disneyland Paris. During school holidays and peak travel seasons, standard queue times at these attractions can stretch to several hours. Pre-booking your attractions tickets is the single easiest way to save time and frustration on any international trip.

Ques. What is the best way to explore Kuala Lumpur?

Ans. Start with the KL Hop-On Hop-Off Pass, which covers the Petronas Twin Towers, KL Tower and all the key cultural areas across the city. Add Aquaria KLCC if you are travelling with family, or head up to Genting SkyWorlds for a full theme park day in the highlands. Activities and tours in Kuala Lumpur offer some of the best value of any destination in Southeast Asia, making it a brilliant choice for Indian travellers on a mid-range budget.

Ques. What types of tours and tickets does MakeMyTrip offer?

Ans. MakeMyTrip offers a wide range of tours and tickets including skip-the-line entry, fast-track access, guided city and day tours, river and dinner cruises, hop-on hop-off bus passes, multi-day theme park passes, combo tickets for multiple attractions and activity-based experiences like safaris, water sports, island trips and cultural shows - across all featured cities and 50+ destinations worldwide.

Ques. Can I book day tours from Paris through MakeMyTrip?

Ans. Yes. In addition to city-based experiences, MakeMyTrip offers guided day tours from Paris to Versailles and other nearby destinations. The Big Bus Hop-On Hop-Off Tour covers central Paris comprehensively. Evening Seine River cruises are among the most popular Paris bookings on the platform and are best reserved ahead of your travel dates.

Ans. Same-day tickets are available for select attractions, but availability is not guaranteed. The Burj Khalifa, Louvre, teamLab Planets and Tokyo DisneySea regularly sell out days in advance, especially during peak season and school holidays. Pre-booking on MakeMyTrip before you travel is the safest way to secure your spot.

City-Wise Guide to Top Tours & Attractions

Dubai, UAE

Dubai is one of those cities that genuinely has to be seen to be believed. The skyline is unlike anything else, the desert is right on the doorstep, and the experiences here range from heart-pounding to jaw-dropping. Activities and tours in Dubai book up quickly, especially during the winter season. Do not leave these off your list:

  • Burj Khalifa - Level 124 & 125 Fast Track Entry - skip the queue and go straight to the top of the world's tallest building.
  • Desert Safari - Dune bashing, camel rides and a traditional dinner under the stars.
  • Global Village - Food, shopping and culture from 90+ countries (open November to April only - do not miss it).
  • Museum of the Future - The most visually stunning building in Dubai, with exhibits that will genuinely blow your mind.
  • Miracle Garden - 150 million flowers in displays you will not believe are real.

Best for: Luxury seekers, adventure lovers and families. Fast-track Burj Khalifa tickets and desert safari are the two activities everyone regrets skipping.

Singapore

Small city, enormous experiences. Singapore consistently ranks as one of the top destinations in Asia for a reason - it amazes every single time. It is the go-to for activities to do with kids, but honestly, adults have just as much fun. These are the experiences everyone is talking about:

  • Universal Studios Singapore - World-class rides and shows that rival any park on the planet.
  • Gardens by the Bay - The Supertrees are even more spectacular in person than in every photo you have seen.
  • Singapore Oceanarium - One of the largest aquariums in the world, endlessly fascinating for all ages.
  • Wings of Time - An outdoor light, laser and water show at Sentosa that genuinely takes your breath away.
  • Night Safari - The world's first nocturnal wildlife park; there is nothing else like this anywhere.

Best for: Families and first-timers. Grab combo tickets for multiple Sentosa attractions and make a full day of it.

Paris, France

Paris has some of the most visited tourist attractions on the planet - and that means crowds. The travellers who plan ahead, book skip-the-line attractions tickets early and go with a solid itinerary have a completely different experience from those who do not. Here is what deserves a spot on your list:

  • Eiffel Tower Tour - You cannot come to Paris and not do this; book ahead or queue for hours.
  • Louvre Museum - Home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of other masterpieces; skip-the-line tickets are non-negotiable.
  • Seine River Cruise Tickets - Notre-Dame, the Eiffel Tower and the grand bridges of Paris, all from the water.
  • Paris Evening Sightseeing Cruise - Paris at night from the Seine is one of the most romantic sights in the world.
  • Disneyland Paris - Europe's most popular theme park; skip-the-line access makes a huge difference.
  • Big Bus Hop-On Hop-Off Tour - The most flexible way to cover all of Paris across one or two days.

Best for: Culture lovers, couples and families. Book everything in advance - Paris does not reward last-minute planning.

Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok never slows down and there is always something new to discover. From the river to the rooftops, from wildlife parks to interactive digital experiences, the activities to do here span every interest and budget. These are the ones worth booking:

  • Chao Phraya Princess Cruise - Dinner, cultural performances and the Bangkok skyline reflected on the river.
  • Safari World Bangkok - A drive-through safari and marine park that makes for a brilliant full day out.
  • Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World - An immersive underwater world in the heart of the city, great for all ages.
  • King Power Mahanakhon Skywalk - Stand on a glass floor at the top of Thailand's tallest building. Yes, it is as scary as it sounds.
  • Space and Time Cube - A futuristic interactive experience that is unlike anything else in Bangkok.

Best for: Culture lovers, families and budget-conscious travellers. Safari World is the top pick for a family day out.

London, United Kingdom

London is one of those cities where you always run out of time before you run out of things to do. It is packed with world-class activities to do with family, legendary landmarks and day trips that could each be a holiday in their own right. Start with these:

  • London Eye - The most iconic view in London; worth every minute, especially at sunset.
  • Big Bus Hop-On Hop-Off Tour - Cover Buckingham Palace, Big Ben, the Tower of London and more, all at your own pace.
  • Madame Tussauds - Always a hit with families and first-time visitors; book ahead to skip the entrance queue.
  • Stonehenge - One of the world's great unsolved mysteries; a guided day trip from London makes it effortless.
  • Windsor Castle and Bath Full Day Guided Tour - Royal palaces and Roman history packed into one unforgettable day.
  • BritRail Passes - The smartest way to explore Scotland, Wales and the English countryside without the hassle.

Best for: History enthusiasts, families and those who want to go beyond the city. BritRail Passes are a must if you plan to travel outside London.

Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo is the kind of city that makes you feel like you have stepped into the future. There is nowhere else on earth quite like it and the range of activities here - from ancient temples to digital art museums to world-record theme parks - is genuinely staggering. Do not leave without experiencing:

  • teamLab Planets Tokyo - A walk-through digital art museum that must be seen to be believed; it sells out weeks in advance.
  • Mount Fuji Full Day Sightseeing Tour - Japan's most iconic landmark, seen properly with a guided tour.
  • Tokyo Skytree - Jaw-dropping views over one of the largest cities in the world.
  • Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo - The Making of Harry Potter; a dream for fans and endlessly impressive for everyone else.
  • Tokyo DisneySea - Rated one of the best theme parks on the planet; lives up to every bit of the hype.

Best for: Families, theme park lovers and culture seekers. Book teamLab Planets and DisneySea as early as possible - these sell out fast.

Phuket, Thailand

Phuket offers far more than beautiful beaches. The things to do here range from world-famous island-hopping tours to spectacular cultural shows and wildlife experiences. It is one of those destinations that rewards every type of traveller and the best experiences fill up quickly:

  • Phi Phi, Maya Bay and Khai Island Day Trip - The most iconic island-hopping experience in all of Thailand; book before you land.
  • James Bond Island Trip - The dramatic limestone karsts of Phang Nga Bay are every bit as breathtaking as they look on screen.
  • Yona Beach Club - A premium beach day with great food, incredible views and a genuinely luxurious feel.
  • Tiger Park and Big Buddha - Combine a wildlife encounter with one of Phuket's most sacred spiritual sites.
  • Carnival Magic and Phuket Fantasea - Two of the best cultural evening shows in Southeast Asia.

Best for: Beach lovers, couples and adventure seekers. The Phi Phi Island Day Trip is Phuket's most-booked experience - do not leave it to chance.

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Kuala Lumpur is one of the best-value international destinations for Indian travellers - and it is criminally underrated. The city is easy to get around, affordable and packed with brilliant activities and tours across culture, family entertainment and adventure. Here is what to book:

  • Petronas Twin Towers - The skybridge and observation deck at one of the world's most photographed landmarks.
  • Aquaria KLCC - A world-class aquarium right beneath the Twin Towers; brilliant for families.
  • KL Tower - Stunning city views at a fraction of the Twin Towers price.
  • Genting SkyWorlds Theme Park - A world-class theme park up in the cool Genting Highlands; a full day out like no other.
  • KL Hop-On Hop-Off Pass - The smartest, most flexible way to see the city if it is your first visit.
  • Legoland Malaysia - A dedicated Lego theme park that younger kids absolutely love.

Best for: Families and first-time international travellers. Activities and tours in KL are some of the best value in Southeast Asia.

Travel Tips Before You Go

Best Time to Visit

  • Dubai and Phuket are best visited between October and April when the weather is cooler and drier.
  • Singapore is most pleasant from February to April.
  • Paris is ideal in spring (April to June) and early autumn (September to October).
  • Bangkok is best between November and February, while London shines from May to September.
  • For Tokyo, aim for cherry blossom season in March to May or autumn foliage in October to November.
  • Kuala Lumpur is drier between May to July and December to February.

How Long to Stay

  • Dubai and Singapore would easily need 4-5 days each.
  • Paris and Tokyo need at least 5-6 days to experience the best tourist attractions properly.
  • Bangkok and Phuket work well as 3-5 days trip with plenty of activities and tours to fill every day.
  • London deserves a full week, especially if you plan to explore beyond the city centre.
  • Kuala Lumpur can be covered comfortably in 2-3 days.
